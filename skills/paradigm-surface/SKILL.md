# Paradigm Surface

Make an evidenced change in technical work understandable and discussable.

Start from the author's supplied workflow observation. Without first-person
evidence, use attribution or a framed question; never invent lived experience or
a recent timestamp. Describe what changed from the previous working model, then
name the abstraction if a short label improves comprehension. List only actual
moving parts that explain the change.

A metaphor, era label or historical analogy is optional. State its material limits
and do not let it imply inevitability. If the evidence shows continuity or an
ordinary improvement, say that rather than manufacture a paradigm shift.

Fit the user's format: a grounded opening, the supported old→new distinction and
its implication may be enough. Invite correction or learning where useful without
turning audience uncertainty into a moral failing.
