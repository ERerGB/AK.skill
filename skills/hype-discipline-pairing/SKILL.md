# Hype–Discipline Pairing

Keep a technical forecast and its verification limits in the same argument.

1. Identify the supplied claim, its evidence and what remains a forecast.
2. State the consequential limitation plainly. Connect it to a verification
   practice actually used or explicitly proposed; never invent the author's habit.
3. Explain how that practice constrains the claimed workflow and what should
   remain outside automation. Use a capable-yet-brittle contrast only when supported.
4. If discussing evaluation, distinguish measured results from felt experience.
   Describe a benchmark/task mismatch only with evidence, and say what the proposed
   alternative measurement covers.

Fit this into the user's requested piece. A short post can carry claim, limit and
check in a few sentences; separate sections and a paradox are optional. Avoid
ritual safety lists without a concrete practice, unsupported certainty, or dismissing
measurement because one metric is incomplete.
