# Irreducible Core

Teach an idea through a small, inspectable implementation. State what the example
preserves, omits and depends on; claim absolute minimality only with evidence.

Prefer one runnable file or a small canonical artifact when it exposes the actual
mechanism. Do not hide the core in imports to achieve a low line count. Walk through
its sections in execution/document order, explaining each section's role. Separate
algorithmic requirements from speed and packaging choices.

Provide the artifact, a runnable command or exact access limitation, a matching
section tour, and its boundaries. Link a longer explanation only where useful.
A notebook may be a runnable companion without becoming a second source of truth.
For a larger stack, a sequence of conceptual commits can serve as the curriculum;
keep errata beside the canonical repository. Do not require a video or a public
upload to complete a local teaching request.
